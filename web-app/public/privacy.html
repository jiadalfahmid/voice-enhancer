Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 21, 2026 • Contact: privacy@clearvoice.studio
In short: Your audio never leaves your device. We do not have accounts, ads, trackers, or analytics.

1. Your Audio

When you choose a file, your browser decodes it locally and the AI models run on your device (in your browser, using WebAssembly and Web Workers). The file, the results, and every version you create stay in your device's memory. They are not uploaded, stored on a server, or shared. When you close or reload the page they are gone, unless you downloaded them. The site's security policy (Content Security Policy) strictly blocks the app from sending data to other websites.

2. What We Collect

Nothing about you or your audio. We do not ask for your name, email, account, or location.

3. What the Hosting Provider Sees

The site is delivered as static files by our hosting provider (such as Cloudflare Pages). Like any website host, it may log technical HTTP request data (such as IP address, browser user-agent, and requested file paths) for network operation and security. That data does not include your audio.

4. Data Kept in Your Browser

  • Model files: Downloaded from this site the first time you use a model and cached by your browser HTTP cache so they load faster next time. You can remove them at any time by clearing your browser cache.
  • Local storage & cookies: We do not use cookies, localStorage, indexedDB, or tracking identifiers. No user data is stored on your device.

5. Third Parties

No third-party scripts, fonts, analytics, ads, or social widgets are loaded.

6. Models and Licenses

Enhancement runs locally with open-source models: GTCRN (MIT), DeepFilterNet3 (MIT or Apache-2.0), and DPDFNet (Apache-2.0), using ONNX Runtime Web (MIT). MP3 export uses lamejs (LGPL-3.0).

Authors, licenses, and notices: /THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt

7. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Material changes will be announced on this page.

9. Contact

Questions? Contact: privacy@clearvoice.studio
